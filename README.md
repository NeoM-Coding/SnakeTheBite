# SnakeTheBite（蛇咬宇宙）

丙午年三月初六，Slay the Spire 2 横空出世，盛极一时，天下云集响应，好评如潮。然细细观之，爬塔五勇士，唯一披挂绿袍、身缠布带、头戴牛骨者甚怪，旁人与之言语，皆视若无睹。某日，爬塔路途艰险，忽见一奇异之物，其上书曰："蛇咬——保留，给予敌人 7 层中毒。"众人皆哂笑："保留如此强悍，不若留之为几用啊。"唯那着绿袍者沉默不语，然心中怒气正盛："安东尼，汝乃无君无父之人！"后人以此为典，写成一书，曰——**"Snake the Bite"**。

On the third day of the third lunar month, in the Year of the Fire Horse, Slay the Spire 2 burst onto the scene, taking the world by storm. Players everywhere rallied to it, and praise flowed like a tidal wave. Yet upon closer inspection, among the five brave climbers of the Spire, one stood out as particularly strange—the one clad in green robes, wrapped in bandages, and wearing a cow skull. She paid no heed to anyone who spoke to her. One day, on a perilous ascent, the party stumbled upon a curious card inscribed with the words: "Snake Bite—Retain. Apply 7 Poison to an enemy." The others scoffed, "If 'Retain' is so mighty, why not keep it for ourselves?" Only the green-robed figure remained silent, though fury seethed within her: "Anthony, you are a man without honor or loyalty!" Later generations enshrined this tale in a mod titled—**Snake the Bite**.

## 测试阶段声明

**注意！！！本 MOD 仍然处于测试阶段，有相当一部分的美术资源尚未完善。**

模组目前暂只支持**中文文本**，英文文本将在后续补全。

模组新增了与"蛇"和"蛇咬"相关的 **70+ 卡牌、2 个事件、10 种药水、20+ 个遗物**。

因为目前模组还在开发中，为了方便测试，卡牌基本为**红色牌与无色牌**，请使用**铁甲战士**以获得更好的模组体验！

**ATTENTION!!!** This mod is currently in the testing phase. A significant portion of art assets have yet to be completed.

The mod currently supports **Chinese text only**. English localization is planned for future updates.

The mod adds **50+ cards** related to "Snake" and "SnakeBite", **2 events**, **8 potions**, and **6 relics**.

As the mod is still under active development, cards are primarily **Red and Colorless** for easier testing. Please play as the **Ironclad** for the best mod experience!

## 食用前注意事项

- 本 Mod 有两个版本，下载时请务必**选对版本**！
- 本 Mod 依赖 **[BaseLib](https://github.com/Alchyr/BaseLib-StS2)** v3.1.6 或以上版本。
- 把 BaseLib  和 SnakeTheBite（注意选对版本！）文件夹放到塔 2 的 `mods` 文件夹中，打开游戏就可以食用啦~
